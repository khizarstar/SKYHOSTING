§01

Purpose & Scope

This policy sets the standard of conduct, activity, and professionalism expected of every SKY HOSTING staff member — from Trial Staff through Official Manager — while on duty or acting in any staff capacity.

It supplements, and does not replace, the Staff Policy governing role grants and approvals. Where the two documents overlap, the stricter standard applies.

§02

Conduct in Staff-Only Areas

Staff-only channels are working spaces, not social ones. Meme culture and low-effort behavior are prohibited while on duty, regardless of tenure or role.

✕ Excessive joking
✕ Low-effort messages
✕ Spam
✕ GIF chains
✕ Reaction spam
✕ "Brainrot" slang
✕ Unrelated memes
✕ Off-topic derailing
EXCEPTION

Casual conversation is permitted only in designated off-topic channels, and must never interfere with active support or moderation duties.

Staff represent SKY HOSTING at all times while in staff spaces. Tone, language, and behavior should reflect a company customers and applicants can trust.

§03

Support Activity Requirements

Activity is measured by meaningful contribution, not by presence or claim count.

5

Minimum Daily Tickets

Every active Support Staff member must resolve or meaningfully assist with a minimum of five (5) support tickets per day.

  • ✓A ticket counts only when staff provide a useful solution, troubleshooting step, or successful escalation.
  • ✕Claiming a ticket without helping does not count toward the minimum.
  • ✕Artificially inflating ticket counts is considered misconduct and is subject to §08 enforcement.
  • ✓Staff should prioritize quality over speed while still meeting the daily minimum whenever ticket volume allows.
  • ✓If fewer than five tickets are available during a shift, staff should assist with all available tickets instead — no penalty applies.
§04

Professional Conduct Standards

  • ✓Be respectful, patient, and unbiased with every customer and applicant.
  • ✕Never argue with customers, regardless of how a conversation escalates.
  • ✕Never misuse staff permissions, immunities, or bypasses granted under the Staff Policy.
  • ✓Always follow internal procedures — improvisation around process is not permitted.
§05

Confidentiality

Staff must maintain confidentiality regarding customer information and internal staff discussions at all times. Information encountered in the course of duty is not to be shared outside its intended channel, discussed with unauthorized parties, or used for personal benefit.

§06

Staff Role Ceiling

Official Manager is the highest role attainable through the staff track. No staff member progresses beyond it — Owner and Co-Owner are ownership positions held by the founding partners and are not part of the staff promotion path defined in the Staff Policy.

§07

Emergency & Account Recovery

If a staff member loses access to their Discord account, or their account is compromised, lost, or otherwise inaccessible, the following recovery procedure applies. This is the only recognized path back to a staff role — access is never restored informally.

Account lost / compromised
→
Staff Recovery Ticket
→
DM MK | PLAYZ
→
🔑 Recovery Code

Open a Staff Recovery Ticket

Do not attempt to reclaim your role or access through any other channel. Open a Staff Recovery Ticket to begin the process — this creates an auditable record from the first step.

DM MK | PLAYZ Directly

Follow up the ticket with a direct message to MK | PLAYZ for identity verification. Recovery is not processed through any other staff member.

Verification & Recovery Code Issued

Once your identity is confirmed, you will be issued a Recovery Code. Save this code somewhere secure and private — it is tied to you personally, not to any single account.

Future Verification

If anything happens to your account again, this code is what you will be asked to produce to verify your identity and restore access. Losing the code makes future recovery slower and may require additional manual verification.

Security Note

Recovery Codes are personal and confidential. Never share your code with anyone — including other staff — outside of the verification step with MK | PLAYZ. A code shared or leaked should be reported immediately via a new Staff Recovery Ticket.

§08

Enforcement

Violations of this policy escalate in severity. Each stage is documented against the staff member's record.

1ST VIOLATION
Verbal Reminder

An informal reminder of the standard, delivered directly and logged internally.

2ND VIOLATION
Written Warning

A formal written warning is issued and attached to the staff member's record.

3RD VIOLATION
Temporary Suspension

Staff access and role privileges are suspended for a defined period.

CONTINUED / DELIBERATE
Demotion or Removal

Continued violations, or any deliberate abuse of staff privileges, may result in demotion or full removal from the staff team.

§09

Acknowledgement

Holding any staff role at SKY HOSTING constitutes acceptance of this policy in full, alongside the Staff Policy governing role grants and approvals. Staff are expected to review both documents periodically, particularly following any revision.
