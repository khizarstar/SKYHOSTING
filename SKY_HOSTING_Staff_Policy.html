§01

Purpose

This policy governs how staff roles are granted, what each role can do, and how staff are held accountable. It exists directly because of the Manager-role incident — a customer was briefly granted near-admin permissions before it was caught and reversed.

The core rule underneath every clause in this document: no staff or elevated role is ever granted informally, by either owner, ever again. Every grant goes through the process defined in §3, with no exceptions carved out for urgency, trust, or seniority.

Application Submitted

All roles route through the Appy Bot application system — no ad-hoc grants, no verbal exceptions, no "just for now."

Time-Boxed Window

Applicants have a 3-hour window to complete their application once started, tracked with full audit trail: UserId, duration, join date, submitted time.

Auto-Grant on Acceptance

Accepted applicants automatically receive 〈◇〉『⏳ • TRIΛL STΛFF』 — this is the only role Appy Bot assigns without a manual second check.

Manual Review Above Trial Tier

Any role above Trial Staff — Support through Official Manager — requires manual approval from MK | PLAYZ, weighed against real trial performance, not just application answers.

Vouching ≠ Granting

AGASTYA may recommend or vouch for a promotion but cannot grant the role himself — role-editing permissions were removed from his account specifically to enforce this.

§05

Security Stack

Three bots enforce this policy technically, layered so their coverage doesn't overlap. This is the enforcement backbone every role grant above ultimately depends on.

Wick — Anti-Nuke / Security

92% score
  • Anti-Nuke — rate limits on kicks/bans, role/channel creation & deletion, webhook creation (2–6 actions/hr, 45% heat threshold)
  • Auto Quarantine — monitors public roles & channel permissions for unauthorized dangerous grants
  • AutoMod — invite links, NSFW/blacklisted links → ban at 90% heat; profanity/slurs (English + Hindi/Hinglish) → timeout
  • Mentions filter — @everyone/@here at 90% heat, @role at 45%
  • Join Gate — blocks unauthorized bot additions, kicks suspicious/new accounts, blocks invite-link usernames

Permits System

Manager-tier
  • Tied specifically to the Official Manager role — no other role qualifies
  • Grants: Automod Immunity, Report Immunity, full moderation toolkit (purge, verify, sanitize, notes, appeals)
  • Grants: Whitelist bypass for kick/ban/channel creation/role creation/webhooks
  • Does not bypass channel deletion or role deletion — those stay rate-limited even for trusted managers

Dyno — Event Logging

  • Message logs
  • Member logs

  • Moderation logs

Sapphire — Secondary Logging

  • Emojis, Events, Invites, Polls

OPEN GAP — PREMIUM LOCKED

Strict Mode (auto-quarantine for unauthorized admins granting dangerous permissions to any role) is currently off, gated behind Wick Premium. This is the single setting that would have auto-caught the original Manager incident without manual intervention. Until enabled, any "Manage Roles/Channels/Server" grant requires manual review under §3.

§06

Conduct & Enforcement

Staff at any tier may have their role and access suspended or revoked for the following. Any trigger below results in immediate downgrade pending MK | PLAYZ review; repeat or severe violations mean full removal and, where relevant, hosting suspension at AGASTYA's discretion.

01
Time Expiry

Trial periods or fixed-term roles that aren't renewed on schedule.

02
Privilege Abuse

Using Automod Immunity or Whitelist bypass to grant unauthorized access or touch roles/channels outside remit.

03
ToS Violations

Including crypto-mining abuse, reselling access, or sharing panel credentials.

04
Security Violations

Self-granting permissions, adding unauthorized bots, circumventing the §3 approval process.

§07

Account & Panel Security

  • 2FA is mandatory for any account holding Official Manager or above, and for both Pterodactyl panel owners.
  • Staff must never share panel or Discord credentials, under any circumstance.
  • Leftover or test accounts (e.g. server_test) are removed once their purpose is served — never left active.
  • Manual review of any "Manage Roles/Channels/Server" grant remains required until Wick Strict Mode is enabled.
